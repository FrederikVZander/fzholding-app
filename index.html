About

Claude FZ Holding is an internal tool used only by the managing director of FZ Holding ApS and its wholly owned subsidiary A New Adventure ApS (CVR 45691373). It connects the companies' own e-conomic agreements to an AI assistant (Claude by Anthropic) that runs on the director's own computer. It reads accounting data and vouchers, reconciles balances, prepares material for the external accountant, and registers and books entries within rules set by the director. The tool is not sold, published or offered to anyone else, and it has no external users.

There is no separate login, launch or logout page. Access is granted and revoked directly in e-conomic by the owner of each agreement.

End-user license

  • The integration is licensed for internal use by FZ Holding ApS and A New Adventure ApS only.
  • It may not be used on any other e-conomic agreement, resold or distributed.
  • Each company can revoke access at any time in e-conomic, after which no further data is read.
  • All bookings remain in e-conomic, which is the system of record. The companies and their management are responsible for their accounts.

Privacy policy

Data controller: FZ Holding ApS (for its own data) and A New Adventure ApS (for its own data).

Data read via the e-conomic API: chart of accounts, balances, booked entries, journals and vouchers, invoices, customers, suppliers and products. Customer and supplier records may contain names and contact details of business contacts and private customers.

Purpose: bookkeeping, reconciliation, reporting and preparation of annual accounts for the two companies. Data is not used for marketing, profiling or training of AI models.

Who processes the data:

  • Visma e-conomic, as provider of the accounting system.
  • Anthropic, as provider of the AI assistant Claude, which processes the data to produce answers and bookings for the director.
  • Revisionscentret Aabenraa, the companies' external accountant, which may receive reports.

Storage and retention: exports, reconciliations and reports are stored in the companies' own document folder on the director's computer. Accounting material is kept for 5 years after the end of the financial year, as required by the Danish Bookkeeping Act (bogføringsloven), and then deleted. API credentials are stored locally in a protected file and never shared.

Your rights: if you are a customer or supplier and want access to, correction or deletion of your personal data, contact frederik@fzholding.dk. You can also complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet).

Data processing

The integration is operated by the data controllers themselves for their own accounts, so there are no external customers on whose behalf data is processed. FZ Holding ApS does not act as a data processor for anyone through this integration.

The service providers listed above process data under their own terms and conditions with the companies. Questions about data processing can be sent to frederik@fzholding.dk.
